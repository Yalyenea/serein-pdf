Considered essentials

For the way you read.

01

Your reading, uninterrupted.

Keep PDFs in sidebar or titlebar tabs. Follow the outline, search across open documents, and return to where you left off.

Tabs · Outline · Search
02

Keep the thought.

Highlight, underline, strike through, and add comments. Save annotations into the PDF, or export your highlights as Markdown.

Annotations · Markdown export
03

Two pages. One view.

Put two papers side by side, or compare different pages of the same PDF. Each pane keeps its own page and zoom.

Side by side · Stacked
04

A page that feels right.

Choose light, dark, or Rosé Pine themes. Let a focus band follow your reading, or hide the interface for an uninterrupted page.

Paper themes · Reading focus
05

Keep your hands on the keys.

Turn pages with J and K, highlight with A, and find with ⌘F. Customize shortcuts in Settings to fit your habits.

Vim-style navigation · Custom shortcuts
06

A home for your papers.

Browse your PDF folders without moving files. Working in LaTeX or Typst? Clean PDFs reload after compilation while unsaved annotations stay intact.

PDF library · Live reload
⌘

Native to your Mac. Open to you.

Built with Swift, AppKit, and PDFKit. Your documents stay in your folders. The source is yours to study, modify, and share under GPLv3.
