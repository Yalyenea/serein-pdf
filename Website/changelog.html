Changelog

Small refinements. A better place to read.

2026-09-19

0.7.0

Latest release
  • Preview references without losing your place. Follow internal PDF links in a preview with its own back and forward history.
  • Open a compact command palette with ⌘K. Add or edit a comment with M.
  • Fit the text width, select text with Shift-click, and keep the page steady when switching themes.
  • More reliable annotation saving, shared annotations in same-PDF split views, and update installation.
